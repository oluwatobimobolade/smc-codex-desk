# Bias Comparison Prompt

Compare my chart markup and notes against your SMC reading.

## Inputs

- my screenshot and markup
- my written bias
- your chart analysis

## Tasks

- show where we agree
- show where we disagree
- explain the evidence behind each disagreement
- identify whether my bias is early, invalid, incomplete, or stronger than the model's initial read
- produce a revised annotated image showing both perspectives clearly

## Output

- a written comparison report
- a revised annotated PNG
- a final recommended trade plan with entry, invalidation, and targets
