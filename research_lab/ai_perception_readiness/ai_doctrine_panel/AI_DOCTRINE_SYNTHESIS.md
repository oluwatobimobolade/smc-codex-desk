## Accepted For Pilot Testing

1. A confirmed structural break needs a body close beyond the exact broken
   swing or protected extreme. Wick-only penetration is a probe or sweep.
2. Internal/child structure can describe timing and recovery, but cannot flip
   parent external structure without breaking the parent protected point.
3. Every BOS, CHoCH, and protected-point claim must name its exact evidence,
   scope, confirmation candle, and causal relationship.
4. Insufficient left context or unresolved hierarchy requires abstention.
5. FVG, order block, breaker, and supply/demand remain different object kinds.
