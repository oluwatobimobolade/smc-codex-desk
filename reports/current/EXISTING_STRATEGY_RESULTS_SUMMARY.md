# Existing Strategy Results Summary

Status: initial summary, not a full evidence audit.

## Known Result Themes

- Broad open-rule SMC tests did not prove robust holdout edge after costs.
- Narrow FVG-width research looked better in some train/holdout slices but was
  mostly Watch/geometry research, not validated live execution.
- 50-trade audit showed replay reproducibility but did not validate a live
  executable strategy, especially under 10 bps costs.
- Fixed high win-rate and universal 3R claims remain unsupported.
- Current ML/prediction modules are scaffolding and not active authority.

## Required Next Evidence

RASC-SMC-V1 must generate its own event set, baselines, cost stress,
walk-forward results, holdout results, and replication reports before any
promotion discussion.
