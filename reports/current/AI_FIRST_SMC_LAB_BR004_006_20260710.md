# AI-First SMC Lab: What Is Ready

## The Working Model

```text
certified candles and formal graph
  -> AI reads clean charts
  -> AI reconciles only certified objects
  -> AI builds and attacks the structure narrative
  -> AI selects sparse professional annotations
  -> deterministic validator and renderer enforce the result
```

AI is now the routine SMC colleague. It can research doctrine, read the clean
chart, connect the story across timeframes, decide what is important enough to
draw, and challenge its own conclusion. It cannot change candle facts, move a
level, turn a wick into a break, overrule parent structure, or produce a trade
authority.

## What The BTC Demonstration Proved

On the reviewed BTCUSDT 4H/1H clean charts, AI recognized the major bearish 4H
expansion and the lower-timeframe recovery. The graph supplied the decisive
fact: the 1H bullish break was older than the later 4H bearish break and had not
broken the parent protected high. The correct result was a mixed, thesis-only
state, two sparse reference marks, and no trade box.

## The Research Result

The AI doctrine panel accepted five conservative pilot rules:

- Body-close confirmation over the exact protected swing; wick-only events stay probes/sweeps.
- Child/internal structure cannot flip parent external structure without proof.
- Every structure claim must identify the object, confirmation, scope, and causal link.
- Unclear hierarchy requires abstention rather than forced classification.
- FVG, OB, breaker, and supply/demand stay separate object kinds.

Displacement thresholds and deeper-origin-OB priority remain visible competing
hypotheses. They are not silently embedded as truth.

## Current Boundary

This is a validated local AI-first research lab, not a validated trading edge.
The next proof is a protected blind cohort and repeated frozen evaluation. Human
review can then certify a small set of rules or disagreement cases, without
having to manually run the whole colleague every day.
