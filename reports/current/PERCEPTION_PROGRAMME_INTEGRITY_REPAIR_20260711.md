# Perception Programme Integrity Repair - 2026-07-11

## What Was Started

The work added a proposed SMC constitution, five-generator candidate atlas,
anchor-preserving AI context, protected-point and active-range state machines,
multi-horizon sweep/breakout lifecycles, POI and inducement research, strict
validators, and synthetic/metamorphic/counterfactual harnesses.

That is the correct shape for the next research layer. It is an attempt to
make structure interpretation causal, explicit, replayable, AI-assisted, and
abstention-aware rather than a pile of independent detector labels.

## What The Deep Check Found

The first version was not ready for authority. The end-to-end runner did not
run the atlas or AI panel, the actual formal graph schema was only partially
understood, empty and ghost interpretations could certify, FVG direction and
pivot availability were wrong, protected-point and range logic contained
causal errors, and synthetic fixtures were described too strongly.

The original diagnosis remains at
`reports/current/PERCEPTION_PROGRAMME_DEEP_AUDIT_20260711.md`.

## What Is True Now

The defects are repaired and covered by adversarial tests. The system now has
one schema adapter, a fail-closed doctrine loader, causal candidate timing,
actual graph-anchor retrieval, candle-replayed structural states, strict
interpretation certification, a genuine six-role programme path, and one
deterministic SMC annotation vocabulary.

The AI remains central where it should be: selecting relevant evidence,
building the parent/child causal story, preserving alternatives, planning a
sparse chart, and criticizing the result. Deterministic code remains central
where AI must not improvise: candles, timestamps, coordinates, object identity,
invariants, label semantics, and authority gates.

## Current Decision

`VALIDATED_LOCAL_EXPERIMENTAL_OBSERVE_ONLY`

The repaired programme may continue as a local research lab. It must not yet
be promoted to expert perception, predictive authority, paper trading, or live
execution. The Constitution is still proposed, all 14 contested decisions
remain unresolved, and no blind adjudicated case cohort exists.
