# Market Structure Constitution V2

Status: **proposed research doctrine; no execution authority**.

This Constitution fixes the semantic layer before detector replacement. It is
deliberately stricter than V1 and does not silently approve its own contested
thresholds.

## Four Layers

1. **Core market structure:** completed-candle facts, candidate swings,
   internal/external structure, protected points, break lifecycle, active
   ranges, and liquidity interactions.
2. **Optional ICT execution doctrine:** order-block/FVG entry models, breakers,
   mitigation blocks, and OTE. These are not universal structure truth.
3. **Strategy timing:** kill zones, sessions, news filters, RR, and risk.
4. **Annotation vocabulary:** labels and chart grammar; communication only.

## Structure Logic

- The first direction-establishing break is `INITIAL_DIRECTION_BREAK`, never
  BOS by default.
- CHoCH is an early **internal** opposite transition.
- MSS is an **external** reversal candidate/confirmation.
- A child CHoCH cannot flip its parent. The parent changes only when its causal
  protected narrative is invalidated by accepted external evidence.
- A wick through a level is a `WICK_PROBE`. A body close creates a
  `BREAKOUT_CANDIDATE`. External acceptance additionally requires normalized
  penetration, displacement, and follow-through or a valid retest.

## POI Logic

An OB can be a last opposing candle, origin cluster, or lower-timeframe
refinement. It becomes eligible only when it predates and causally owns an
accepted break, matches direction/scope, remains lifecycle-active, and is
range-coherent. A lower-timeframe object inside a parent origin refines the
parent; it does not replace it. FVG-only pockets remain secondary without
supporting OB or confirmation.

The three ranking axes remain separate:

- deterministic eligibility;
- AI pairwise semantic rank;
- empirical reaction score, which is unavailable until labeled outcomes.

No rule guarantees a future reaction.

## Annotation Logic

- Draw local swing-to-break segments, not page-wide BOS/CHoCH lines.
- BOS must state direction and scope.
- CHoCH must be internal; MSS must be external and lifecycle-qualified.
- POIs show type and lifecycle.
- Inducement is `IDM_HYPOTHESIS`, never a fact.
- Trade boxes require `TRADE_PLAN_READY`.
- The visual critic can clean or downgrade, never promote.

## Authority

V2 is hash-sealed but not approved. It cannot alter canonical V2 detection,
authorize a signal, prove predictive edge, or enable paper/live execution.
Promotion requires independent adjudication and a blind benchmark.
