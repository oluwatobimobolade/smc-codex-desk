## Purpose

Strengthen the system's ability to reject its own plausible but unsupported
SMC interpretation without relying on a human to catch each runtime error.
This package improves implementation consistency and refusal quality; it does
not claim empirical perception accuracy, predictive edge, or profitability.

## Implemented controls

- Hash-sealed autonomous truth constitution with a clean-room reference
  oracle and production-claim adapter.
- Parameter-envelope conformance certificates, market microstructure
  integrity checks, regime observations, and a selective-outcome ledger.
- Volume evidence carried through order-block detection and POI authority.
- Same-timeframe causal reconciliation, including timeframe-qualified event
  identities and exact source-candidate/body-close binding.
- Reclaim-safe two-stage MSS confirmation: a sufficiently deep direct
  protected-level probe may establish parent invalidation context, while only
  a later independently displaced and followed-through break can confirm the
  MSS; any intervening body-close reclaim cancels the chain.
- V3-disputed structure and POI objects withheld from official and native
  charts while accepted historical context remains descriptive.
- Data-failed timeframes suppressed after the effective post-gap context
  policy, rather than being trusted from raw row counts.
- Metamorphic price transforms bind transformed float observations back to
  their exact source observations, so harmless forex float tails cannot create
  false scale, translation, or mirror conflicts.
- Old unexplained forex gaps may reduce conformance scope only to a recorded
  latest-contiguous segment and only when the required timeframe depth remains.
- Forex daily charts are reconstructed from the declared closed hourly source
  at the New York 17:00 wall-clock boundary, including daylight-saving
  transitions, instead of trusting geometrically inconsistent standalone
  provider daily candles.
- Market source-identity certification. Explicit proxies such as `GC=F` for
  requested `XAUUSD` retain diagnostic candles but receive no structure,
  range, POI, entry, stop, target, memory-comparison, or chart authority.
- Clean review banners that name source failure or structure reconciliation
  and explicitly deny entry/SL/TP.
- Timeframe-honest native labels: historical same-timeframe zones are `Prior`
  context, refinements are `Refined` context, and neither inherits entry
  authority merely by being visible.
