## What Is Operational

1. Protected benchmark partitions: doctrine examples, development cases, blind
   validation, and annotation comprehension. The blind area has a hash-chained
   access ledger, rejects training/tuning/prompt-development access, and can
   open only for a frozen final evaluation.
2. Public benchmark pilot: one BTCUSDT development pack and one GBPUSD
   annotation pack are registered as `AI_WEAK_LABELS`. No blind case or human
   gold label was invented.
3. Governed AI lab: six strict roles are staged in causal order. The blind
   reader cannot see detector IDs; later roles must use certified evidence IDs.
   Each role writes its prompt, input hash, raw JSON, parsed JSON, provider
   mode, and audit. Repair is bounded to one attempt.
4. AI doctrine panel: local project sources were reviewed by four distinct AI
   roles. Five conservative rules are pilot-ready, two important claims remain
   contested, and AI consensus cannot modify the detector or become gold truth.
5. Manual AI-assisted BTCUSDT run: the clean 4H and 1H charts were reviewed,
   then the six-role path was run as `MANUAL_AI_ASSISTED_JSON`. It identified a
   bearish 1D/4H parent, a stale 1H bullish recovery, no certified POI, and a
   sparse no-trade annotation plan. The formal graph remained authoritative.
