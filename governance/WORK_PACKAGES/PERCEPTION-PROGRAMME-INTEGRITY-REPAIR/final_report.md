# Perception Programme Integrity Repair - Final Report

## Verdict

`VALIDATED_LOCAL_EXPERIMENTAL_OBSERVE_ONLY`

The started perception programme is real, substantial, and now materially
safer. The original eight commits created the right research categories, but
the first implementation could certify empty or ghost interpretations, used
several incompatible schemas, contained causal and market-direction errors,
and presented a caller-supplied interpretation as an end-to-end run.

Those defects are repaired. Promotion remains blocked because the doctrine is
still proposed and no blind adjudicated market cohort exists.

## Completed Repair

- Doctrine loading is hash-required, schema/version checked, status controlled,
  and authoritative only after explicit approval of every contested decision.
- Candidate IDs, availability times, FVG direction, fractal confirmation, and
  local displacement origins now obey one compatibility contract.
- The retriever reads the actual formal graph and preserves protected points,
  range endpoints, breaks, HTF POIs, and active liquidity anchors.
- Protected points are directionally opposing, candle-replayed, causally
  ranked, and ambiguous ties abstain instead of sorting by identifier.
- Active-range geometry, hierarchy, level interactions, inducement waiting
  states, and POI rank consensus were repaired.
- Validators now fail closed on empty outputs, ghost role evidence, future
  availability, graph invariant failures, low-priced narrative claims, and
  unsealed overrides.
- The programme runner can build a real multi-timeframe atlas, execute all six
  governed roles, invoke the annotation-render boundary, and certify only the
  resulting evidence-grounded interpretation.
- Unimplemented retrieval tools are no longer advertised as executable. The
  runtime uses anchor-preserving context until a bounded tool loop exists.
- Annotation labels are deterministic and explicit: external/internal BOS and
  CHoCH, protected high/low, OB, FVG, EQH/EQL, and IDM cannot be renamed by AI.
- Synthetic cases are described as contract fixtures, never expert or human
  gold truth.
